Mouse and keys

Click
Put the caret there and hear the moment under it. On the time row, anywhere along it
Alt-click
Another caret; on one, take it away. A paste, or M, goes to every caret
Drag
Select a time range; on the spectrogram, a time and frequency box, and with Shift one axis of it, the other whole. An edge snaps to the cue or marker it comes near, which lights
Double-click
Select between the cues either side (the hits found, and the markers); in a pause, the pause
Triple-click
Select the phrase: a sentence, a line played, between longer pauses
Shift-click or drag
Extend the selection. Shift while dragging keeps to one axis; while dragging the caret, makes a range from where it was
Drag a selection
Move its audio there; the rest closes up after it
Alt-drag a selection
Copy its audio over what is there
The corners at its top
Inward: fade it in from its start, out to its end. Outward: a fade centred on that edge. A click: the fade's curve
The pill on its top edge
Up or down: its level; on the spectrogram, its pitch. ⌘-drag its body does the same
The grip in its bottom right corner
Across: its length, pitch kept, what follows moving with it. ⌘-drag its end does the same
Alt-drag elsewhere, ⌘D
Another range; the next one like the selection. Edits act on all
A cue on the time row
Drag it: the audio either side stretches to meet it
Hold or drag the caret or an edge
Hear the moment under it; near a cue, it goes onto it
Space
Play or pause: the selection, else from the caret. Put the caret elsewhere while it plays and it plays from there
Arrows, ⌥ arrows, ⌘ arrows
Move the caret a little, to the next cue, to the next phrase; Shift extends
Wheel
Scroll: through time; up and down over zoomed-in frequencies, through them. Pinch, or Ctrl and the wheel, zooms the axis under the pointer: the frequencies or the spectrum beside them, the levels (a quiet sound larger, or what goes over full scale), else time
= − 0
Zoom in, out, show all (all the frequencies, full scale)
Delete, ⌘X ⌘C ⌘V, ⌘Z
Edit the selection; undo. Each edit is a step of the chain
B
Hear the file as it opened, level-matched, at the same place; B again, the output
Record
Into the waveform from the caret, over what is there, as a tape; into a selection, to its end (with the loop on, pass after pass). Undo takes it back
M
A marker at the caret; drag its flag to move it, double-click it to take it away
Tools
Every method, by kind: a click adds it to the chain, on the selection if there is one
Edits
The chain's steps. Choose one: the output up to it, and its settings; again, or Esc, the whole chain. Its Δ: what it takes out; its eye turns it off
Export
The output checked against a delivery spec, rule by rule; its format, and the file
View settings
Above the axes: the frequency scale, what shows over the sound, snapping, the units of time and of level
Tabs
Above the picture, a sound each, its own script and edits. A sound opened goes into a tab of its own; + a new one, × closes one
F10
The menu
